# AGENTS.md: Kaggriculture Research Notes & Knowledge Base (`kaggriculture/notes/`)

This directory houses the empirical insights, strategic playbook, and telemetry notes generated by **Dream-RSI** across multi-stage arena epochs.

---

## 1. Directory Structure

- `STRATEGY_PLAYBOOK.md`: The living, progressive playbook of optimal strategies, opening books, market dynamics, and failure modes discovered by LLM agents.
- `NOTE_epoch_*.md`: Chronological debriefs produced by Dream-RSI at the conclusion of each tournament epoch, analyzing DuckDB match traces.

---

## 2. Epistemic Principles

- **Empirical Grounding**: Every tactical recommendation must cite match telemetry from `arena.duckdb` (win margins, crop yield data, bank compounding curves).
- **Dialectical Refinement**: When a hypothesis fails in competition, document the pathology and record how the prompt personality was adapted.
